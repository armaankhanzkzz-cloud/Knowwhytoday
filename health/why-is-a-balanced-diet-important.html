Why Is a Balanced Diet Important?

Health

A balanced diet provides the body with a variety of nutrients needed for energy, growth, tissue repair, and normal physiological functions.

What does a balanced diet include?

It generally includes a variety of vegetables and fruits, whole grains, protein-rich foods, and appropriate amounts of healthy fats, while limiting excess added sugars, salt, and highly processed foods.
