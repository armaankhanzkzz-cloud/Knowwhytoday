What Was Democracy in Ancient Athens?

History

Ancient Athens developed a form of direct democracy in which eligible citizens participated personally in political decision-making rather than choosing representatives for every decision.

Was Athenian democracy fully inclusive?

No. Women, enslaved people, and many residents without citizenship were excluded from political participation, so it differed substantially from modern universal suffrage.
