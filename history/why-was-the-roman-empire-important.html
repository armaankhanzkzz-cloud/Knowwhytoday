Why Was the Roman Empire Important?

History

Rome influenced law, government, engineering, language, military organisation, and urban development across a vast territory.

What helped Rome govern such a large empire?

Roads, administrative institutions, military organisation, taxation, and communication networks helped connect and manage its territories.
