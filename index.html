Editorial Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Know Why Today is a learning website built around one simple goal: make worthwhile questions easier to understand. Our articles are written for general educational use and are designed to be clear, focused, and readable without unnecessary complexity.

Our editorial principles

  • Accuracy: explanations should reflect reliable knowledge and avoid presenting uncertain claims as established fact.
  • Clarity: technical ideas should be explained in plain language without oversimplifying the core meaning.
  • Context: articles should explain not only what something is, but why it matters when that context improves understanding.
  • Originality: articles should be written as original explanations and avoid repetitive templates, duplicated wording, copied passages, and generic filler.
  • Corrections: factual or editorial errors may be corrected whenever they are identified.

Educational scope

Know Why Today covers subjects such as science, psychology, philosophy, technology, health, history, geography, business, and economics. The site is intended for learning and general information rather than professional advice.

Editorial independence

Educational explanations should not be changed simply to satisfy advertisers, sponsors, or commercial interests. If monetisation is introduced later, it should remain separate from the editorial conclusions of the articles.

Questions, corrections, or editorial concerns can be submitted through the Contact page.

Sources Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Know Why Today researches educational topics using information from external websites and other relevant resources. Our aim is to make information easier to understand while respecting the rights and usage conditions of the original sources.

How we choose sources

We aim to use relevant and dependable sources, which may include educational institutions, government organisations, academic or scientific resources, established publications, museums, recognised reference websites, and other appropriate sources for the subject being explained.

Respect for source permissions and copyright

We aim to consult websites and resources that make information available for legitimate reference, research, or educational use and to respect any applicable copyright notices, terms of use, licences, and other restrictions. Listing a source does not give Know Why Today permission to copy material that is otherwise protected.

Original explanations

Know Why Today articles are intended to be original explanations. Information gathered during research should be understood, summarised, and expressed in our own words and structure. We do not intentionally reproduce substantial portions of another website's protected text, images, or other material without appropriate permission or rights.

Sources at the end of articles

When external websites or resources are used to research an article, relevant sources should be listed at the end of that article so readers can see which websites or materials were consulted and can explore the topic further.

Source transparency

The inclusion of a source does not mean that the source endorses Know Why Today, and it does not mean that Know Why Today endorses every statement or opinion published by that source. Sources should be selected for their relevance to the particular information being explained.

Updates and corrections

Sources and explanations may be reviewed when information changes, an error is identified, or a stronger or more current source becomes available. Questions about sources or corrections can be submitted through the Contact page.
