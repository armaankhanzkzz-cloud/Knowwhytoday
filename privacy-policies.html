English Idioms & Phrases

App: English Idioms & Phrases
Developer: Know Why Today
Privacy contact: armaankhanzkzz@gmail.com

1. Introduction

English Idioms & Phrases is an educational application designed to help users learn and practice English idioms and phrases. Know Why Today respects your privacy and aims to minimize unnecessary collection of information.

2. Information We Collect

The current version does not require an account and does not ask users to provide personal information such as their name, address, phone number, or email address. The app does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information to the developer or third parties.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The app may store certain preferences and app-related information locally on your device, including favorite idioms, quiz-related progress or scores, reading/page position, font-size preference, quiz-volume preference, and dark-mode preference. This information is used to provide the app's features and remember your settings.

4. How We Use Information

Locally stored information is used only for the application's functionality and preferences. It is not used to identify you personally.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. The current version does not intentionally transmit the locally stored preferences described above to the developer or third-party services.

6. Internet and External Services

The current application provides its main idiom, search, favorites, settings, and quiz functionality without requiring an account or transmitting personal information to an external server. The current app does not intentionally include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally remains on your device until it is removed by the app, cleared by you, the app's data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. You can generally remove locally stored app data through your Android device's application settings.

8. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It does not require users to create accounts or provide personal information.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information. Because the app primarily stores preferences locally, users should also use the security features provided by their device.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the app's functionality or data practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of English Idioms & Phrases, contact armaankhanzkzz@gmail.com.
