Why Do We See Lightning Before Hearing Thunder?

Science

Lightning and thunder are produced by the same storm event, but light travels through air enormously faster than sound.

Why is there a delay?

The flash reaches your eyes almost immediately, while the sound wave from the lightning takes longer to travel through the atmosphere. The longer the delay, the farther away the lightning is.
