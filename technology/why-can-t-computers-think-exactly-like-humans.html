Why Can't Computers Think Exactly Like Humans?

Technology

Computers can perform remarkable calculations and recognize complex patterns, but human intelligence involves perception, memory, emotions, social understanding, physical experience, and many other abilities.

What is the fundamental difference?

A computer follows computational processes designed by its software and learned parameters. Human cognition arises from a living biological system. Computers can imitate parts of human behavior without necessarily reproducing the full human experience.
